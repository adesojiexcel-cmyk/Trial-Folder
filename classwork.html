Why Learn About Cat Breeds?

cats comes in dozens of recognized breeds, each with its own look, personality, and history. This page covers just three to keep them simple: the siamese, the persian, and the miane coon.

Every cat breed tells a story about the place and people who shaped it.

siamese

The siamese is one of the oldest and recognizable cat breed, orginally from thailand(fromerly known as siam) They are known for their striking blue eyes, short coat, and very vocal personalities.

Quick facts

  1. origin Thailand
  2. coat:short, with colour "points" on ears, face, paws and tail
  3. Personality: Talkative, social, and affectionate
  4. Lifespan 12-20 years
